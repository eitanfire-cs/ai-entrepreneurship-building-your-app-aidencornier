The problem

People need a way to choose the most efficient transportation to reach their destinations. They want to know which option is fastest, cheapest, or most environmentally friendly.

Who has it

People who need to travel to a certain place and want to arrive on time.

What my app does today

RouteWise helps users compare different transportation options. Currently, users can enter DIA (Denver International Airport) to see different types of transportation and their estimated travel times. The app includes categories for the fastest, cheapest, and greenest options.

The travel time estimates are not yet fully accurate for different destinations. I attempted to integrate Google Maps to improve accuracy but ran out of time. I also manually changed the heading in App.jsx to focus on DIA because the estimates were primarily set up for that destination.

What testers told me

The destinations were not working after users clicked the Find My Route button.

The app did not show which transportation options were fastest or provide time estimates.

The app displayed the same estimated travel time regardless of the destination entered.

What people use now instead

People use Google maps instead.
